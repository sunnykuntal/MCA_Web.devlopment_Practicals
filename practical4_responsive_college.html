About the college

Learn with purpose.

Coder Yatra is built around a simple idea: students learn best when academic knowledge is connected to practical experience.

Strong fundamentals. Practical experience.

Our programmes focus on developing the knowledge and confidence students need to work in a technology-driven world.

From programming and software development to data, artificial intelligence and cybersecurity, learning is supported by projects and practical activities.

  • Industry-oriented curriculum
  • Project and practical learning
  • Experienced faculty and mentors
  • Career and placement guidance
